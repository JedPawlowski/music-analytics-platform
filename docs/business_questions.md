# Business Questions

## Listening Behaviour

- Who are my most played artists?
- Which genres dominate each month?
- What are my listening peaks by hour/day?
- What are my longest listening sessions?
- How many sessions do I have?
- What is my average session length?

## Engagement

- Which tracks are skipped most often?
- What percentage of tracks are fully played?

## Trends

- How has my music taste evolved over time?
- Which artists became more popular for me recently?
